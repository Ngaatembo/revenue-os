Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

In short. Revenue OS helps owner-run businesses see which customers are waiting for a reply and which invoices are overdue, using the owner's Gmail and Google Sheets. It asks Google for read-only access and never sends, edits or deletes your email or spreadsheets.

From Gmail it stores the other person's address and name, the subject, a short preview (up to 200 characters) and dates. It does not request message bodies or attachments. You can disconnect at any time and choose to delete what was already synced. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use Google data to train AI models.

3. Google data: exactly what we access and store

Revenue OS connects to Google only when an owner or admin of a workspace chooses to connect. Access is read-only. It asks Google for permission through Google's own consent screen, and you can withdraw it at any time (see section 9).

Gmail

Gmail access, from the application's code
Permissions requestedhttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly (Gmail read-only access), plus openid and email so Revenue OS knows which Google account was connected.
What that permission allowsGoogle's read-only Gmail permission is broader than what Revenue OS uses: technically it would allow reading entire messages, attachments and settings. Revenue OS is built not to do that. It cannot send, change, label, archive or delete anything with this permission.
What Revenue OS asks Gmail forThe connected mailbox's address; a list of message identifiers (for the first sync, messages from the last 90 days, excluding Promotions, Social, Forums, Spam, Trash and chats); then, for each message, only its identifiers, thread identifier, labels, date, a short preview (Gmail's "snippet"), and these headers: From, To, Subject, List-Unsubscribe, Precedence and Auto-Submitted. Afterwards it asks Gmail which new messages arrived and fetches the same fields for them. Drafts, chats, Spam, Trash, Promotions, Social and Forums messages are skipped and nothing is stored from them.
What it storesFor each message: the other party's email address and display name, the subject (up to 300 characters), the preview (up to 200 characters), the date, whether you sent or received it, a flag for whether it looks automated, the message and thread identifiers, and the connected mailbox's address.
What it does not store or requestMessage bodies, attachments, or the full headers. The List-Unsubscribe, Precedence and Auto-Submitted headers are used only to decide whether a message looks automated; their values are not stored. Because the subject and preview are parts of your emails, they can contain personal or confidential information, and Revenue OS stores them.
What it is used forTo show which customers wrote and have not had a reply, which conversations went quiet after you wrote last, and a link back to the original email in Gmail.

Google Sheets and Google Drive

Google Sheets and Drive access, from the application's code
Permissions requestedhttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.readonly, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly, plus openid and email.
What Revenue OS asks Google forFrom Drive, only a list of your spreadsheets (identifier, name, type, last-modified time and link) so you can choose one, and the last-modified time of the chosen spreadsheet to notice changes. From Sheets, the title and tab names of the chosen spreadsheet and the cell values of the tab you select. It cannot read other Drive files and cannot edit anything.
What it storesThe chosen spreadsheet's identifier, name and tab; the invoice rows imported from it (customer identifier and name, invoice number, currency, amounts, issue and due dates); a fingerprint of the imported content used to avoid duplicates; and synchronisation status.
What it is used forTo find overdue invoices and keep them up to date without manual uploads.

Signing in with Google

7. Token security and access controls

  • Google refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held as a secret in the application's server environment. The key is not stored in the database or sent to browsers. A token is decrypted only inside server code, when a background job or action needs to talk to Google.
  • Where tokens are kept: the table holding connection credentials has row-level security enforced and grants no access to browsers or signed-in users. Only the server component can read it.
  • Short-lived access tokens are obtained from Google when needed and are not written to the database.
  • Sign-in handshake: the one-time state value used while connecting Google is stored hashed and expires after 10 minutes.
  • Workspace isolation: every workspace's data is protected by database row-level security so one workspace's members cannot read another's.
  • Roles: only an owner or admin can connect or disconnect a Google account; owners, admins and operators can change follow-up tracking; viewers can only read.
  • Traffic between your browser and Revenue OS uses HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim any security certification. We describe only protections that exist in the product today.

8. How long we keep information

These are the behaviours that actually exist today.

  • Google access tokens (refresh tokens): removed from our database immediately when you disconnect, or when Google tells us you revoked access.
  • Synced Gmail information: kept for as long as the workspace exists, unless you choose "Disconnect and delete synced email data" (section 9) or ask us to delete it. There is no automatic expiry: the first sync reads the last 90 days, and new messages keep being added while Gmail stays connected.
  • Invoices imported from Google Sheets or uploaded files: kept for as long as the workspace exists. Disconnecting Google Sheets stops further reading but does not delete invoices already imported.
  • Your notes, follow-up marks and other workspace records: kept for as long as the workspace exists.
  • Sign-in handshake values: expire after 10 minutes.
  • Provider logs: kept according to Cloudflare's and Supabase's own settings and policies.

We do not currently run a scheduled job that deletes old data. Deleting a whole workspace or account is done on request (section 10).

9. Disconnecting, revoking and deleting

  • Disconnect Gmail (Connections page): Revenue OS stops reading the mailbox, removes its stored access, and asks Google to revoke the permission. You choose either to keep the information already synced, or to delete the synced email data, which removes the stored messages' details, your follow-up marks and notes on those conversations, and your hidden-sender list for that workspace, and resets the synchronisation so a later reconnection starts afresh.
  • Disconnect Google Sheets: Revenue OS stops reading the spreadsheet, removes its stored access, and asks Google to revoke the permission. Invoices already imported are kept.
  • Revoke from Google: you can remove Revenue OS at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When Google reports that access was revoked, Revenue OS removes its stored token and shows that you need to reconnect. Information already synced stays until you delete it or ask us to.
  • Revocation by Google: while the Revenue OS Google app is in testing, Google expires connections about every 7 days and you will be asked to reconnect.
